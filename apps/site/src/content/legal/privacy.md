## In short

- Riparti does not use cookies.
- There are no accounts, advertising, profiling, or visit analytics.
- The trip you are editing stays in your browser.
- Maps, address search, and route calculation rely on the third-party services
  listed below.

## Cookies

Riparti sets no first-party cookies and loads no third-party scripts that set
them. That is why the site shows no consent banner: there are no cookies or
tracking tools to authorise.

Some preferences and the trip in progress are stored in the browser's
`localStorage`. That is technical data, not cookies, and it is never sent to
Riparti.

## Data stored in your browser

| Key                      | Content                                        |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| `backseat.theme`         | Light or dark theme                            |
| `backseat.language`      | Interface language                             |
| `backseat.trip`          | Passengers, legs, prices, and tolls of the trip |
| `backseat.paypalHandle`  | PayPal.Me handle used for payment requests     |

These values stay on the device until you clear the site data in your browser.
The site operator cannot read them.

## Third-party services

When you use the map or search for an address, your browser contacts these
services directly:

- **OpenStreetMap** (`tile.openstreetmap.org`) — serves the map tiles and
  receives your IP address and the area you are viewing.
- **Photon** (`photon.komoot.io`) — receives the text you type into the address
  search.
- **Valhalla** (`valhalla1.openstreetmap.de`) — receives the coordinates used to
  calculate the route.
- **PayPal.Me** — contacted only when you click a payment request link; PayPal
  applies its own notice.

These services receive your IP address and the data the request needs. Riparti
does not receive or store it.
